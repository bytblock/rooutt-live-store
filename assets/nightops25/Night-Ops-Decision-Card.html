Night Ops Decision Card

One page. Three columns. Ship before dawn.

Keep

  • What already makes or protects cash this week
  • What a buyer already asked for twice
  • What you can deliver in under 2 hours with tools you own

Kill

  • Ideas that need new capital, unverified accounts, or someone else’s permission
  • Anything that only works if a stranger DMs you first
  • Busywork that feels like progress (logo, rename, reorg)

Automate

  • Repeat answers → one saved reply / checklist
  • File delivery after payment → template + Cashtag note match
  • Status beats every 30 minutes while a job is open

Cash rule (tonight)

Floor is real dollars in Cash App — not “interest.” If it doesn’t clear $rooutt with a matching note, it didn’t happen.

How to use

  1. Write 3 bullets under each column before you open a laptop rabbit hole.
  2. Pick ONE Keep item. Ship it before dawn.
  3. Ignore Kill. Schedule Automate for after first cash clears.
